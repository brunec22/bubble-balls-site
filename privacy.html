Bubble Balls Classic

Privacy policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Bubble Balls Classic is developed by Alen Brunec, Slovenia.

No personal data collection

The game does not collect or transmit personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share personal data.

On-device storage

Your best score and sound preference are saved locally on your device. These values are not sent to the developer. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Children

The same no-collection practices apply to all players, including children.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. This is separate from the game. This page contains no analytics or tracking scripts. Any hosting provider's technical logs are governed by that provider's practices.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change.
