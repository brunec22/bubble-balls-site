Bubble Balls Classic

Support

A colorful tap-to-jump arcade game by Alen Brunec.

How to play

Your ball moves automatically. Tap to jump, collect purple hearts and avoid black bars and spiky hazards. Collect every heart in a scene to continue. Your best score is stored on your device. Use the speaker button to toggle sound.

Need help?

Contact support@abdigitech.net. Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

The game has no ads, accounts or tracking and collects no personal data.
